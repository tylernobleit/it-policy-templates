# Acceptable Use Policy

**Company:** [COMPANY NAME]
**Effective date:** [DATE]
**Owner:** [NAME, TITLE]
**Next review:** [DATE]

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains how company computers, accounts, networks and data may be used, so that the business, its customers and its employees stay protected.

## 2. Who it applies to

All employees, contractors and anyone else using [COMPANY NAME] systems or data, on company-owned or personal devices.

## 3. General rules

- Use company systems primarily for business purposes. Limited, reasonable personal use is allowed if it does not affect work, security or legality.
- You are responsible for activity under your account. Do not share your password or let others use your account.
- Do not attempt to bypass security controls, access data you are not authorised to see, or install unapproved software.
- Report lost or stolen devices, suspected phishing and security concerns right away (see section 9).

## 4. Accounts and passwords

- Use a unique password of at least [12] characters for each work account; a password manager is [required/recommended].
- Multi-factor authentication must be enabled wherever available.
- Never give your password or MFA codes to anyone, including someone claiming to be IT. IT will never ask for your password.

## 5. Email and internet

- Be cautious with links and attachments, especially unexpected ones. Report suspicious messages instead of replying.
- Do not use company email for illegal, harassing, discriminatory or offensive content.
- Do not send sensitive information (customer data, financial details, credentials) by ordinary email unless it is protected as described in section 6.

## 6. Data handling

- Store business files only in approved locations ([e.g., SharePoint/OneDrive, file server]). Do not keep the only copy on a local drive or personal cloud account.
- Sensitive data ([DEFINE: customer records, financial data, employee data]) must be encrypted when stored on portable devices and shared only with people who need it.
- Do not copy company data to personal devices or removable media unless approved.

## 7. Devices

- Lock your screen when you step away; devices auto-lock after [10] minutes.
- Keep operating systems and software updated; do not disable antivirus, firewall or encryption.
- Personal devices used for work must meet [minimum requirements: current OS, screen lock, encryption] and may need to be enrolled in device management.
- Return all company devices and data when employment or the contract ends.

## 8. Monitoring

Company systems may be monitored and logged for security, maintenance and legal purposes, as permitted by law. Users should not expect privacy on company systems. [REVIEW WITH LEGAL COUNSEL FOR YOUR JURISDICTION.]

## 9. Reporting

Report immediately to [IT CONTACT, PHONE, EMAIL]:

- Lost or stolen devices
- Suspected phishing or account compromise
- Malware, unusual device behaviour, or unauthorised access
- Accidental disclosure of data

Prompt reporting is never penalised; delay makes incidents worse.

## 10. Consequences

Violations may lead to disciplinary action up to and including termination, and legal action where appropriate.
